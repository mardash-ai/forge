Governance on writes (C29)

Low-risk writes auto-run; anything leaving the account (real email, calendar mutations seen by others) stages an approval a human resolves. The approval floor is enforced in dispatch — not a convention.

Households & visibility (C31)

Roles owner/adult/teen/assistant with 13 permission tokens; resource visibility private/group/shared with reader/writer grants; teens+assistants get resource.share_up — they can share TO the owner only, and the wider step is the owner's.
